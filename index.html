About

I hold a Professional Master's in Geomatics from the Faculty of Sciences of Tunis. Day to day I classify LiDAR point clouds of electrical networks, build 3D models of infrastructure and check the quality of geospatial data before delivery.

I also measure ground movement with Sentinel-1 radar interferometry and build GIS models for hazard assessment, from slope and rainfall to land use and drainage.

  • GIS and mapping

    Spatial analysis in QGIS and ArcGIS, and maps built to be read and used.

  • Radar and InSAR

    Ground displacement from Sentinel-1, from SLC imagery to line-of-sight results.

  • LiDAR and 3D

    Point cloud classification, quality control and 3D models of infrastructure.

        Project

        Master's thesis: landslide assessment with Sentinel-1 InSAR and GIS

        Ghardimaou area, Jendouba, north-western Tunisia

        A geospatial study of landslide susceptibility and ground displacement, combining radar interferometry with a GIS model.

        • Sentinel-1 SLC imagery prepared in SNAP, then DInSAR and time-series processing with PyGMTSAR in Google Colab.
        • DEM data used for terrain correction; displacement analysed along the radar line of sight (LOS).
        • GIS susceptibility model built from slope, rainfall, lithology and permeability, land use, drainage network and roads.
        • Multi-criteria analysis (MCA) and weighted overlay to rank areas by relative susceptibility.
        • InSAR and GIS outputs combined to support landslide monitoring, hazard assessment and environmental risk management.
